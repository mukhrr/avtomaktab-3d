# M5a, the Sign Set, Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every one of the 297 signs and plates in Uzbekistan's Road Traffic Rules exists in the game as data and as art that looks like the real sign, and the signs already in the city stand on real-style shared poles with plates under them.

**Architecture:** The sign catalogue moves into the repo as validated data keyed by the Uzbek sign number. Art comes from public-domain Wikimedia Commons SVGs of CIS signs, renumbered to Uzbek numbers, plus hand-drawn SVGs for Uzbek-only signs; a build tool rasterises them with `sharp` into one texture atlas. `Sign` switches from a 10-value `type` to the catalogue number `code`, and the renderer draws every sign as an alpha-tested quad from that atlas, two draw calls in total.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5.6, three 0.169, Vitest 2.1, tsx tools, `sharp` (already a dev dependency) for SVG rasterising, the Wikimedia Commons API.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-m5-road-signs-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Uzbek sign numbers everywhere (service signs 6.x, plates 7.x). Source: https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-5953883, Appendix 1.
- Uzbek names are official; Russian and English names are translations and are marked `translated: true`.
- Only Commons files whose licence is public domain or CC0 are used. Each one's Commons page and licence are recorded in the data and in `CREDITS.md`.
- Source SVGs fetched from Commons sit in `assets-src/signs/` (gitignored) and are only read by tools, never executed. Hand-drawn SVGs are committed in `data/signs/drawn/`.
- Signs add at most 4 draw calls in total. Sign art adds at most 4 MB to the download and at most 48 MB of GPU memory.
- High preset on Mukher's Mac: at most 8 ms frame time in the chase view, at most 120 main-pass draw calls.
- An unchecked sign may ship but is never penalised (M5c reads `checked`).
- At most 3 signs per pole, plates not counted.
- Commits in Mukher's name only, never a `Co-Authored-By` line or a "Generated with Claude Code" line. Commit on `main`, never push.

## Review Focus

1. A sign in `signs.json` whose code or value has no atlas cell: the build must fail with the sign id, never draw a blank or wrong plate. Test in Task 5.
2. Two signs at the same spot for opposite directions of a two-way road must stand on two poles, one per side. Test in Task 6.
3. The atlas fails to load (404, decode error): the game still starts, without signs, and logs one error. Test in Task 6.
4. Mipmap bleeding: at distance a sign must not pick up colour from its neighbour cell. Cells get padding; test in Task 5.
5. The existing examiner and AI behaviour for stop, give way, priority, no entry and turn bans must not change with the code migration. The existing suites cover it; Task 2 must keep every one of them passing unchanged except for the field rename.

| File | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `data/signs/catalogue.json` | The 297 entries: number, group, names, shape, size, value kind, meaning, art source, reference, checked, exempt |
| `data/signs/drawn/*.svg` | Hand-drawn SVGs for Uzbek-only signs and gaps |

| `src/world/signMount.ts` | Pure placement: groups signs onto poles, stacks them, hangs plates |
| `src/world/signs.ts` | Loads the atlas and draws poles and sign quads |
