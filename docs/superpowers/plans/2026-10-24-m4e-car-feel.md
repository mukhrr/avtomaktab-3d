# M4e Car Feel Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the player's car feel like a car:
- Sounds: engine that follows rpm and load, tyre squeal, indicator click, horn, belt chime, start and stall.
- A Chevrolet Gentra (Lacetti) body that leans when braking, accelerating and cornering.
- A cockpit with a dashboard, working gauges, a steering wheel that turns, and A-pillars.

**Architecture:**
- `src/audio/carAudio.ts` builds a Web Audio graph on top of M4d's `Sfx` and is updated once per frame from the drivetrain, cabin and wheel state.
- The player model reuses M4d's `carGeometry('lacetti')` (the Gentra/Lacetti sedan) inside a visual-only pivot (`bodyLean.ts`) driven by smoothed accelerations.
- `src/vehicle/cockpit.ts` builds an interior on its own layer, which only the cockpit camera shows. Gauges are canvas textures redrawn at 15 Hz.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5.6, three 0.169.0, Web Audio API, Vitest 2.1.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-m4-realism-design.md` (Goal 4, "Car feel"; "Sound" in The how and in Settled after the builder check).

## Global Constraints

- The player car is a Chevrolet Gentra (Lacetti) by Mukher's choice. Its physics box (4.48 x 1.74 m), drivetrain (1.5 L, similar power) and mirror and eye positions stay as they are; the visual body changes. The HUD and help text say Gentra where they name the car.

- **No downloads.** All sound is synthesised. The anti-goal is an engine that drones or annoys within five minutes:
  - several harmonics with load-dependent filtering
  - slight random variation
  - a master volume and a mute
- **Physics and rules stay unchanged.**
  - Physics, vehicle handling and anything the rules read stay as they are; the lean is visual only.
  - The cabin logic (indicators, belt, glances) stays the same; sounds only listen to it.
  - The M3 exam runs must pass unchanged.
- **Audio starts only from the Start click.** Sound has an on/off and volume setting on the start screen, stored with try/catch localStorage, with i18n keys in uz and ru.
- **Budgets on the reference Mac.** The cockpit view on high stays within 8 ms gpu p95 and 120 main-pass calls. The chase view gains at most +3 calls. Audio work is at most 0.3 ms per frame on the main thread.
- **Cockpit geometry must not block the mirror views** or the view of the road ahead, from the bonnet line up to the horizon. The mirrors keep their current positions.
- **House rules.** Comments only for what code cannot say. No em or en dashes. Commits by Mukher with no Co-Authored-By or "Generated with" lines.

### Task 1: Car audio engine

**Files:**
- Create: `src/audio/carAudio.ts`, `src/audio/carAudio.test.ts`
- Modify: `src/audio/sfx.ts` (from M4d; shared master bus)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `class CarAudio { constructor(ctx: AudioContext, master: GainNode); update(s: CarSound, dt: number): void; horn(on: boolean): void; event(e: 'start' | 'stall' | 'click' | 'chime'): void; fade(to: number, seconds: number): void; dispose(): void }`, where `CarSound = { rpm: number; throttle: number; running: boolean; speed: number; slip: number; gear: number }`.
- **Engine:**
  - The base frequency is the firing frequency, `rpm / 60 * 2` for a 4-cylinder.
  - Three oscillators (1×, 2× and 0.5× the base) are mixed through a low-pass filter. The cutoff runs from 600 Hz at idle with no throttle to 3,500 Hz at full throttle and high rpm.
  - Noise for combustion texture, through a band-pass filter.
  - Gain from 0.15 at idle to 0.5 at high load.
  - A slow ±1.5% pitch wobble at idle.
  - Frequency and gain are always smoothed with `setTargetAtTime` (time constant 0.05 s).
- **Start and stall:** a 0.6 s starter whirr (noise plus 12 Hz amplitude modulation) on start. On stall, the rpm sweeps down to 0 over 0.4 s.
- **Tyres:** band-passed noise at 900 Hz, with gain proportional to `max(0, slip - 0.15)` and capped. `slip` is the largest wheel side-impulse ratio, or longitudinal slip under hard braking, read from the Rapier vehicle controller.
- **Click:** a 25 ms filtered tick on each indicator on/off edge, synchronised to the cabin's blink phase.
- **Chime:** a 0.4 s two-tone ding, repeated every 2 s while the speed is above 10 km/h and the belt is off. The chime is cosmetic; the `noSeatBelt` rule is unchanged.
- **Horn:** the player horn uses M4d's horn voice while held. The key is KeyN; check that `src/vehicle/input.ts` doesn't already use it. The horn has no effect on scoring.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.** Use a stub AudioContext that records nodes and the targets of `setTargetAtTime` and `setValueAtTime`. Test that:
  - idle maps to base 26.7 Hz and cutoff 600 Hz, and 6200 rpm at full throttle maps to cutoff 3,500 Hz
  - with the engine off, the gain target is 0
  - a stall schedules a frequency ramp to 0 within 0.4 s
  - slip 0.1 gives tyre gain 0, and slip 0.6 gives more than 0
  - the chime fires every 2 s while unbelted above 10 km/h, and never below that speed
  - `dispose` disconnects every node it created

### Task 3: Player Gentra body and lean

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/vehicle/carModel.ts`, `src/vehicle/vehicle.ts` (expose accelerations if needed), `README.md` (say Chevrolet Gentra instead of Cobalt style)
- Create: `src/vehicle/bodyLean.ts`, `src/vehicle/bodyLean.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- The player model uses `carGeometry('lacetti')` from M4d (Gentra sedan; refine its silhouette here if needed: rounded nose, sloping rear window, short high boot, Gentra grille) with white paint and the existing wheels and headlights, built as one `body` group inside a `lean` pivot. The wheels stay outside the pivot.
- Produces: `class BodyLean { update(longAccel: number, latAccel: number, dt: number): { pitch: number; roll: number }; reset(): void }`.
  - A spring-damper with a frequency of 1.6 Hz and damping 0.6.
  - Pitch is 0.5° per m/s² (nose down when braking), clamped to 3°.
  - Roll is 0.6° per m/s² (outward in a corner), clamped to 4°.
  - Accelerations are the chassis's own local accelerations, smoothed over 0.1 s.
- `resetToLane` and the exam start call `reset()`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - Steady braking at −5 m/s² settles to a 2.5° nose-down pitch.
  - Steady cornering at 6 m/s² settles to a 3.6° roll toward the outside.
  - 15 m/s² clamps to 3° and 4°.
  - `reset` returns to zero immediately.
  - A step input overshoots by at most 15%.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run.** Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** and wire it in. Confirm `car.upright` still reads the physics quaternion.

**Interfaces:**
- `COCKPIT_LAYER = 2` is enabled only in cockpit mode, alongside today's rule that the cockpit camera hides `CAR_LAYER`.
- Produces: `createCockpit(): { root: THREE.Group; update(s: { speedKmh: number; rpm: number; indicator: Indicator | null; blinkOn: boolean; hazard: boolean; belt: boolean; steer: number; running: boolean }): void }`, attached to the car object.
  - **Dashboard:** a dark dashboard slab and an instrument binnacle in front of the driver's eye (`DRIVER_EYE` in `cameras.ts`, driver on +x).

  - **Steering wheel:** rotates by `steer × 450°` around its column axis.
