# M4d Traffic Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make Tashkent traffic look and behave like Tashkent traffic:
- low-poly models of the cars actually on the road (Cobalt, Nexia, Lacetti, Spark, Damas, taxi, city bus) in realistic paint colours
- pedestrians with a walking body
- AI cars that change lanes with their indicator on
- short horns when the player blocks a green light

**Architecture:**
- Car classes and paint weights live in `src/traffic/carClasses.ts`.
- `src/traffic/carModels.ts` builds one low-poly geometry per class: a painted body part and a fixed part (glass, tyres, lights) with vertex colours.
- `trafficView` draws them instanced, with brake and indicator lamps.
- `TrafficSim` gains a MOBIL lane-change step on its own seeded random stream, and exposes the lane-change indicator on `AgentView`.
- A small Web Audio synth module (`src/audio/sfx.ts`) plays horns. M4e extends it for the player's car.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5.6, three 0.169.0, Web Audio API, Vitest 2.1.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-m4-realism-design.md` (Goal 3, "Traffic"; "AI lane changes" in The how).

## Global Constraints

- No downloads. Models and sounds are generated in code.
- Nothing the rules read may change for the player. AI behaviour does change, so:
  - The M3 headless exam runs must still pass: careful driver on seed 1 scores 0 to 10 points, the red-light runner fails with only `redLight`, and the no-indicator counts are unchanged.
  - Diagnostic seeds 3, 7, 9 and 11 must still finish, with their scores reported.
  - If AI behaviour changes a seed's outcome, the test driver is fixed, not the rules (spec ruling).
- AI lane changes use their own seeded random stream, so spawn and route draws are unchanged.
- Visual car models stay inside the traffic box (`CAR_CLASSES` length and width, height up to 1.6 m for cars and 3.2 m for the bus). Physics bodies and collision boxes keep their sizes.
- Budgets on the reference Mac:
  - High: at most 8 ms gpu p95 and 120 main-pass calls.
  - Low: at most +6 calls over before M4d.
  - Traffic and pedestrians together: at most 10 main-pass calls.
- Audio starts only after the Start click, which counts as the user gesture.
- Comments only for what code cannot say. No em or en dashes. Commits by Mukher with no Co-Authored-By or "Generated with" lines.

## Review Focus

1. **AI lane change into the player.** Expected:
   - An AI car never changes into a gap the player occupies or will occupy within 2 s at current speeds.
   - It never changes lanes inside a junction or within 30 m of a stop line.
   - The player's collision fault logic still puts fault on the car that drove into the other.

   Task 3 tests these.
2. **Lane changes and junction priority.** A car changing lanes keeps or releases its reservation, so it never holds two lanes at once and never deadlocks. Expected: the gridlock tests and seeds still finish. Task 3 tests this.
3. **Horn spam.** Expected:
   - At most one horn per AI car per stop.
   - Never within 2 s of the light turning green.
   - Never while the player is moving faster than 2 m/s.
   - The horn volume falls with distance.

   Task 4 tests the rule.
4. **Draw calls with seven classes.** Expected: all classes share instanced meshes. The budget is 10 calls for traffic plus pedestrians. Task 2 tests this.

**Interfaces:**

    - **bus:** a city bus with a long window band, a door gap and a stripe.

  Hidden or unused instances get zero scale. Cars cast shadows.

### Task 3: AI lane changes

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/traffic/trafficSim.ts`, `src/traffic/lanes.ts` (adjacent-lane helpers if missing)
- Create: `src/traffic/laneChange.ts`, `src/traffic/laneChange.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `mobil(input: { a: number; aNew: number; followerOld: { a: number; aNew: number } | null; followerNew: { a: number; aNew: number } | null; bias: number }): boolean`, the MOBIL incentive and safety check.
  - Politeness 0.3, threshold 0.2 m/s², safe braking −4 m/s².
  - `bias` is +0.6 toward the lane the route needs for the next turn and −0.6 away from it.
- Lane-change state per agent:
  - `changing: { from: LaneRef; to: LaneRef; t: number; dur: number } | null` with `dur` 3 s.
  - Indicator on for 2.5 s before the move starts. The move is a lateral blend over `dur`.
  - `AgentView` gains `indicator: 'left' | 'right' | null`.
- Rules:
  - Consider a change at most once per 1.5 s per agent, using the lane-change rng stream (seed + 1000).
  - Only on lanes of the same edge and direction.
  - Never within 30 m of the lane end when that end has a stop line, signal or junction, never on a connector, and never while braking hard (decel above 3 m/s²).
  - The gap check includes the player as a follower or leader in the target lane, using the player's position and speed.
- Reservations: the agent stays in its old lane for gap checks until it is past halfway, then belongs to the new lane only. Junction reservations are only taken on the final lane.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - `mobil` decides correctly in the textbook cases: a faster free lane gives a change; an unsafe follower brake gives no change; a turn bias forces a change toward the turn lane.
  - In a 2-lane test network with a slow leader, a faster agent changes lanes after showing its indicator for 2.5 s.
  - No change happens within 30 m of a stop line.
  - No change into a gap where the player is within 2 s.
  - Seeds 1, 3, 7, 9 and 11 run 600 s headless without gridlock, despawning stuck cars only by the existing rules.
  - The exam runs still pass, and diagnostic seed scores are reported.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run.** Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.**
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** the suite, including `examRun`. Expected: PASS. If a careful-driver seed now fails because an AI car cuts in, first check the lane-change safety rule against the player. Fix the test driver only if the AI move was legal and safe.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** `feat(traffic): AI lane changes with indicators`.

### Task 4: Horns

**Files:**
- Create: `src/audio/sfx.ts`, `src/audio/sfx.test.ts`, `src/traffic/horn.ts`, `src/traffic/horn.test.ts`
- Modify: `src/main.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `class Sfx { constructor(ctx: AudioContext); horn(volume: number, pan: number): void; dispose(): void }`, a synthesised car horn. It uses two detuned square or saw oscillators at about 400 and 500 Hz through a band-pass filter with a short attack and release, for 0.35 s, or a 0.25 s double toot.
  - The AudioContext is created or resumed in the Start click handler.
- Produces: `hornDecision(state: { greenFor: number; playerSpeed: number; playerAhead: boolean; honked: boolean; distance: number }): boolean`.
  - It returns true when the AI car is first in its queue behind the player at a green light, `greenFor` is 2 to 6 s, the player speed is under 2 m/s, and the car hasn't honked during this stop.
  - Volume is `1 / (1 + distance / 10)` and pan comes from the bearing relative to the camera.
- Free drive and exam both honk. A horn has no effect on scoring.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - `hornDecision` covers each condition in turn.
  - `Sfx.horn` schedules oscillators that start and stop within 0.5 s, using a stub AudioContext that records node creation and `start`/`stop` times.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run.** Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** and wire into `main.ts`. Settle the decision per agent once per frame. The sound must not start before the Start click.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** the suite, tsc and build. Expected: PASS.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** `feat(traffic): horns when the player blocks a green light`.
