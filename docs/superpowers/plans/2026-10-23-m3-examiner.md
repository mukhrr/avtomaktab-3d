# Milestone 3: Examiner, Rule Engine and Exam Route Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the living district into a driving exam. You pick Exam, buckle up, and an examiner directs you along a fixed route through central Tashkent in Uzbek or Russian. Every mistake is detected and scored by tier (instant fail / high / low), the drive stops at an instant fail or at 100 points, and a debrief shows the total, the mistakes and where they happened on a map.

**Architecture:** A per-step `DriveSnapshot` (pure data: player pose matched to a lane, inputs, signal and crossing state ahead, nearby AI, events) feeds a set of small pure rule detectors in `src/exam/rules/`, each emitting `Violation`s that reference a penalty table loaded from data. An `Examiner` state machine (pure) follows a route made of lane refs and maneuvers, triggers direction prompts, and ends the drive. Thin adapters play voice clips, draw the exam HUD and debrief in React, and wire everything into `main.ts`. Hand-placed signs and a route file live in the district's data folder. AI traffic gains right-hand priority, give-way/stop signs and left-turn yielding so the player meets correct behaviour.

**Tech Stack:** as M1/M2. Voice placeholders generated offline with `edge-tts` (Python, dev-only tool, not shipped as a dependency).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-avtomaktab-3d-design.md` (Goals 4–6, "Exam realism", "Rule engine", "Examiner", "Debrief", "Calls made after review")
**Builds on:** M1 and M2 on `main` at `57c6751`.

## Facts this plan relies on

- Penalty baseline: the MIT avtodrom simulator (`axadjonovsardorbek/avtodrom-simulator`, `src/sim/penalties.js`) lists 30 entries at 5/10/15/20/25/50/100 points, including red light 100, crash into obstacle or pedestrian 100, left the route 100, stall 20, no turn signal 5, stop line 20, speed over limit 5 per 5 s / 100 above 40 km/h, and a 5 km/h speed measurement tolerance.
- Spec: the practical exam passes at 99 points or fewer. Tiers from osonprava.uz: instant fail (red light, not yielding to a pedestrian, entering the oncoming lane), high (no indicator, no mirror check, stopping past the stop line), low (stall, wrong gear).
- M2 exposes: `SignalSystem.approachState(edgeId, dir, s, within?)`, `PedSim.occupied(crossingId)`, `TrafficSim.agents`, `LaneNetwork` (`path`, `exits`, `connector`, `pathS/edgeS`), `EventBus` (`collision` with closing speed, `pedestrianHit` only above 1 m/s), and `nearestLane`. 223 OSM ways carry `maxspeed`.

## Global Constraints

- Pass/fail: total ≥ 100 points fails and stops the drive; any instant-fail violation stops it at once. A total of 99 passes.
- Every penalty entry carries: `id`, `tier` (`fail` = 100, `high` = 20, `low` = 5 unless the table says otherwise), `points`, Uzbek (Latin) and Russian text, and `clause` (YHQ clause number with a source URL) or `clause: null` with `unverified: true`. Never invent a clause number.
- Until a driving instructor reviews the table, every exam screen shows "Amaliy baholash" / "Учебная оценка" ("practice scoring").
- Languages: Uzbek (Latin) and Russian for every exam string and voice line. No string literals for exam text in components: all text comes from `src/i18n/*.json`.
- Speed tolerance 5 km/h on every speed rule.
- Rules are pure functions of snapshots plus their own small state; no rule reads Three or Rapier objects. Deterministic in tests.
- Generated TTS voice is a placeholder: `CREDITS.md` marks it "not cleared for commercial release; replace before launch".
- Keys added: Z / X left/right indicator (toggle, auto-cancel after the turn), V hazard, B seat belt, hold 1 / 2 / 3 to glance at left mirror / rear-view / right mirror. Gamepad: D-pad left/right indicators, D-pad down hazard, left stick click belt, right stick left/up/right glances. Existing bindings stay.
- Comments only for what the code can't say; no em dashes in code, docs or UI text.

## Non-goals (deferred to M4 or later)

- Replay of the drive (debrief shows the map and list only).
- Multiple routes, other districts, route editor UI.
- Recorded human voice, full UI localisation beyond exam screens, menus beyond the minimal start screen.
- Theory test and avtodrom exercises.

## Review Focus

1. **Map-matching ambiguity** (player straddling two lanes, inside a junction, on a parallel service road, briefly off road): the snapshot never flips edges every frame; rules that depend on lane (oncoming, lane choice) only fire after the condition has held for the stated time, and nothing fires while inside a junction polygon except junction rules. Pinned in Task 5.
2. **Double counting:** one physical mistake produces one violation (e.g. a red-light run is not also a stop-line overrun; a crash is one event even if Rapier reports several contacts; speeding repeats only once per 5 s). Pinned in Task 6 and Task 7.
3. **False instant fails** that would make players quit: yellow light when the car could not stop, a pedestrian who steps out when the car is already inside the stopping distance, AI car rear-ending a stationary player, touching a building at under 1 m/s while parking. Each has a test showing no fail. Pinned in Tasks 6–7.
4. **Examiner robustness:** the player misses a turn, reverses, drives off route, stalls, or resets with R. The examiner re-routes (no penalty) for a missed turn, R during an exam ends it as "abandoned", and directions never point at a turn already passed. Pinned in Task 8.
5. **Language completeness:** every violation id and examiner line exists in both `uz` and `ru`, and every voice line has an audio file or a subtitle-only fallback without crashing. Pinned in Task 1 and Task 9.

### Task 6: Rule engine part 1 (signals, signs, speed, signalling, lanes)

**Files:**
- Create: `src/exam/rules/types.ts`, `src/exam/rules/engine.ts`, `src/exam/rules/signals.ts`, `src/exam/rules/signs.ts`, `src/exam/rules/speed.ts`, `src/exam/rules/signalling.ts`, `src/exam/rules/lanes.ts`
- Test: one test file per rules file

**Interfaces:**
- `interface Violation { id: PenaltyId; t: number; position: Vec2; detail?: Record<string, string | number> }`
- `interface Rule { id: string; update(s: DriveSnapshot): Violation[]; reset(): void }`
- `class RuleEngine { constructor(rules: Rule[]); update(s: DriveSnapshot): Violation[] }` (de-duplicates: a `redLight` at time t suppresses `stopLinePast` and `yellowEntry` from the same stop line within 3 s)

Rules (each fires once per occurrence; thresholds exact):
- `redLight`: the car's front crosses a stop line while the approach is red or redYellow. `yellowEntry`: crosses while yellow and at yellow onset `canStopBefore(v, distance, 4)` was true; never when it was false (Review Focus 3).
- `stopLinePast`: the car stops (speed < 0.3 m/s for 1 s) with its front more than 0.5 m past a stop line whose light is red.
- `noFullStopAtStopSign`: passes a stop sign's line without speed < 0.3 m/s within the 10 m before it.
- `noEntry` / `turnRestriction`: entering an edge/dir against a `noEntry` sign, or making a turn a restriction sign forbids at that junction. `wrongWayOneway`: travelling against a oneway edge's allowed direction for more than 1.5 s outside junctions.
- `speeding`: over limit + 5 km/h for 5 s, then again for each further continuous 5 s. `speedingSevere`: over limit + 40 km/h, or over limit + 20 km/h for 3 s.
- `noIndicator`: a maneuver (connector turn > 30°, or a lane change detected by `ref.lane` change outside junctions) without the matching indicator on for ≥ 1 s before the maneuver starts. `noIndicatorPullOff`: starting from rest at the route start without the left indicator. Hazard counts as neither.
- `noMirrorCheck`: same maneuvers without a glance at the matching side mirror (left for left turns and left lane changes; right for right) within the 6 s before; rear-view required before braking from above 30 km/h to a stop is not checked (keep scope).
- `wrongLaneForTurn`: turning left from a lane that isn't the leftmost lane of its direction, or right from a lane that isn't the rightmost, on edges with 2+ lanes in that direction.
- `tailgating`: time headway < 1.0 s to the leader at speed > 20 km/h for 3 s continuous; repeats after 10 s.
- `noSeatBelt`: moving above 5 km/h with the belt off (once per drive).

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests** with scripted snapshot sequences per rule, including the Review Focus 2 and 3 cases: red-light run gives exactly one `redLight` and no `stopLinePast`; yellow when unable to stop gives nothing; speeding for 12 s gives two `speeding`; indicator turned on 0.5 s before a turn gives `noIndicator`; a glance 7 s before gives `noMirrorCheck`, 5 s before gives none.
- [ ] **Step 2–4: RED, implement, GREEN.** **Step 5: Commit** (`feat(exam): rules for signals, signs, speed, signalling and lanes`)

### Task 7: Rule engine part 2 (yielding, position, collisions, vehicle handling)

**Files:**
- Create: `src/exam/rules/yielding.ts`, `src/exam/rules/position.ts`, `src/exam/rules/collisions.ts`, `src/exam/rules/handling.ts`
- Test: one test file per rules file

Rules:
- `pedestrianNotYielded`: the car's front crosses a zebra or a signal crossing (on walk) while `pedOnCarriageway` is true, or while `pedAtCurb` is true and the car was more than its stopping distance (`v²/8 + 2` m) away when the pedestrian started waiting. Not when the pedestrian stepped out inside the stopping distance (Review Focus 3).
- `vehicleNotYielded`: entering an unsignalled junction when a `junctionConflicts` entry with priority over the player has `tta < 3 s` (priority: from the right unless the player is on a `priorityRoad` approach, or the player has a `giveWay`/`stop` sign; on green at a signalled junction, oncoming traffic going straight has priority over the player's left turn).
- `oncomingLane`: `lateral` beyond the centreline by more than 0.9 m (half the car width) on a two-way edge for 1 s outside junctions; dashed centreline overtaking is not modelled, so any crossing counts.
- `stoppedOnCrossing`: stopped (speed < 0.3 m/s for 2 s) with the car's box overlapping a crossing.
- `collisionCar`, `collisionPedestrian`, `collisionObstacle`: from `events`; a crash is debounced per partner for 3 s (Review Focus 2); `collisionCar` is not charged when the player's speed was below 1 m/s and the closing speed came from the AI car (rear-ended while stopped); `collisionObstacle` only above 2 m/s.
- `harshBraking`: deceleration over 6 m/s² for 0.3 s with no obstacle reason (no red light, crossing occupancy or leader within stopping distance); once per 10 s.
- `stall`: `running` goes from true to false (manual only).
- `rollBack`: speed below −0.3 m/s for more than 0.5 m of travel while in a forward gear (or D).

### Task 8: Examiner

**Files:**
- Create: `src/exam/examiner.ts`, `src/exam/directions.ts`
- Test: `src/exam/examiner.test.ts`, `src/exam/directions.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- `type ExamPhase = 'briefing' | 'driving' | 'finished' | 'failed' | 'abandoned'`
- `interface ExamResult { phase: ExamPhase; total: number; violations: Violation[]; durationS: number; distanceM: number; routeId: string; passed: boolean }`
- `interface Prompt { key: string; vars?: Record<string, string | number> }` (i18n keys: `exam.brief`, `exam.start`, `exam.turnLeftAhead`, `exam.turnLeftNow`, `exam.turnRightAhead`, `exam.turnRightNow`, `exam.straight`, `exam.uturn`, `exam.reroute`, `exam.finishAhead`, `exam.stopHere`, `exam.passed`, `exam.failed`, `exam.violation`)
- `class Examiner { constructor(route: ExamRoute, net: LaneNetwork, penalties); start(): void; update(s: DriveSnapshot, violations: Violation[]): Prompt[]; abandon(): void; get result(): ExamResult; get nextManeuver(): { maneuver; distance } | null }`

Behaviour:
- `briefing` until the player presses a key to begin: prompt `exam.brief` (fasten belt, adjust mirrors, indicate before pulling off). Then `driving` with `exam.start`.
- Progress tracking: the current route step advances when the match enters the next step's lane (or its connector). Directions: "ahead" at 50 m before the maneuver's junction (or immediately if closer when the step begins), "now" at 15 m; straight-on prompts only at signalled junctions where turning is possible.
- Missed turn: when the match is on a lane that isn't the expected next step and is past the junction, re-plan from the current lane to the remaining waypoints with `planRoute`, prompt `exam.reroute`, no penalty. Directions never refer to a junction already passed (Review Focus 4).
- Finish: within 15 m of the route end on the right lane at speed < 0.5 m/s for 2 s gives `finished`; `passed = total < 100`.
- Any `fail`-tier violation, or total ≥ 100, ends with `failed` immediately and prompt `exam.failed`.
- R (reset) or leaving the district during an exam gives `abandoned`.
- Each violation produces `exam.violation` with the penalty text (spoken only for high and fail tiers to avoid chatter).

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests** on testNetwork with a scripted snapshot stream: prompts at 50 m and 15 m for a left turn; a missed turn triggers one `exam.reroute` and a new route that reaches the finish; reaching the end and stopping gives `finished` and `passed`; a `redLight` violation ends `failed` at once; 5 `high` violations (100 points) end `failed`; 19 points total over the drive passes; directions never mention a passed junction.
- [ ] **Step 2–4: RED, implement, GREEN.** **Step 5: Commit** (`feat(exam): examiner with directions, rerouting and pass/fail`)
